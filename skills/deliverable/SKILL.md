# Shareable deliverable

This skill owns an explicitly selected recipient-facing HTML product. Ordinary advice, task updates and stored sources do not automatically become HTML. A generated artifact alone does not complete a broader task, authorize sending or authorize publication.

Resolve the actual owner and output destination from the request and current workspace. Read its `AGENTS.md`, applicable output contract and optional `.agents/skill-adapters/deliverable.md`. The adapter supplies actual brand/style sources and persistence/runtime providers. Source location never selects the destination. Missing necessary style or writer capability blocks only that effect; do not substitute a private vault, invent an owner or revive a retired workflow.

## Recipient and purpose

Choose the real recipient and use. A professional group document contains relevant business context, design principles, visuals and decision points. Original making instructions, full chats, received mail and tactical preparation stay at their authorized source owner unless the recipient actually needs and may receive them. Do not conceal such material in a hidden DOM or payload.

A personal question/answer artifact may retain relevant source excerpts when they help its chosen recipient. Distinguish source fact, the user's proposal, an actual accepted decision and an open question. A proposal sent to someone is not that person's accepted agreement.

Sharing-message tools and AI handoff are independent optional features. Include a message-copy section only for a concrete need. Include recipient AI context only if the recipient is meant to continue with their own AI. A normal project-group deliverable contains neither by default; absence also means no hidden payload, script, navigation entry or dormant button.

Read current source material and confirmed corrections first. Keep literal source → human answer/decision → derived artifact traceable without requiring that chain to be a visible label format. Research retrievable facts rather than asking the user to repeat them. Ask only an unresolved material choice; a full authorized execution request does not require another routine approval.

## Select the artifact shape

For reading, comparing, discussing or meeting use, prefer one continuous document. For a process the recipient must actually perform, use the available output step-screen mode when requested/appropriate, retaining its complete no-script fallback. Do not turn a reading document into a wizard merely because it describes steps. If this distinction materially changes the product and context does not decide it, clarify that choice.

An expressly requested shopping artifact uses the available shopping/research contract: original question, evidence, comparison/product grid, actual acquisition options with verified total prices, sources. Research owns factual claims; layout does not invent price, availability or links. Ordinary shopping advice remains chat unless an artifact is requested.

## Build the content

1. Give the document a meaningful title and subject. Include real project/component names, sender/recipient and dates only where they help interpretation. Do not invent codes or a sent date.
2. If a meeting exists, state its absolute date, start/end and participants. Otherwise omit the section. Relative “soon” wording ages badly when forwarded.
3. Open with the recipient's concrete reason to read and the important context or principles. Do not automatically quote the whole incoming request.
4. Organize topics by the actual decision/problem. Choose a decision tree for choices, a step route for execution, a feedback loop for improvement or a relation diagram for dependencies when that makes understanding faster.
5. Put necessary explanation at its visual node with the same names, order, owner and content. Core relations and choices stay visible; native details may hold depth when the selected viewer supports them without scripts. Do not add a second table or caption that merely retells the visual.
6. Keep optional AI context complete, visible and independently copyable when selected; no topic selector or hidden second state. The source and user decisions determine its content, not internal agent logs.

Standardize reusable building blocks, not the user's conversation. Never invent a personal answer, approval, metric, service name or meeting. Unknowns remain unknowns rather than precise-looking placeholders in a final artifact.

## Visual continuity and copy

Use the actual approved existing design or explicitly selected brand source as primary reference. Before extending a previous version, render it and identify its real tokens, typography, rhythm, navigation and composition. Additional content is not permission for redesign. Compare corresponding sections at the same widths; change only what the requested capability requires.

Use semantic headings, readable line length, meaningful arrows/groups and responsive white space. Keep colors restrained and touch actions large enough for the actual context. Follow the available frontend copy/accessibility gate. Explanations must prevent a concrete misreading or support a decision/action; remove filler and duplicate visual narration. Technical logs, local management paths and raw source screenshots stay outside the shared version unless specifically needed and shareable.

## Continuous, static-first sharing

Use one self-contained readable flow with optional ordinary section anchors. A desktop contents list may move above the body on mobile. Do not require custom scroll boxes, fixed document heights, routers, automatic jumps, tabs or previous/next screens to reach core content.

A recipient version is script-free by default: no scripts, inline handlers or javascript URLs. Ordinary fragment and source links remain useful. Put a target for closed depth at its visible summary or leave the target text open; a fragment must not depend on a click-interceptor to expose it. Native details are allowed only when the selected route works without JavaScript and all depth prints completely.

An explicitly requested interactive capability can have a separately identified interactive version where a static recipient version is also needed. Do not ship empty buttons or hidden features to imply capabilities that the recipient cannot use.

## Actual viewer failure and alternatives

Inspect the actual shared file and error when a recipient reports a script/security/viewer problem. A generic error does not prove a specific security product or root cause. Produce the necessary continuous static version; a full PDF with source links can be a requested/scoped delivery alternative. Do not disable security software or claim client acceptance from a local browser pass.

If a logo/diagram format is actually unsupported, export a supported image and preserve recognizable shape. That case does not establish a universal ban on SVG or links. Browser-safe rendering and received mail/Teams/SharePoint viewing are different evidence boundaries.

When PDF is produced, render all pages for completeness, readableness, clipping, sources and absence of PDF JavaScript. Keep its content and calculations identical to the selected HTML state.

## Persistence and versioning

Use the selected owner's actual writer if it is required. Preserve earlier issued versions; do not silently overwrite a sent/released artifact. Record real author, timestamp, skill/source identity and validation evidence through that provider. Do not claim a writer-specific call exists in another vault because an old recipe mentions it.

Keep implementation history/version navigation outside the recipient artifact, including hidden DOM/AI context, unless the recipient explicitly needs that history. A single current artifact link should not carry an unwanted predecessor list. Existing note IDs, status and source activity remain owned by their current writer; creating a document does not change them implicitly.

## Verify and deliver

Run the applicable output plan/check and format gate on final saved bytes. Check real names/dates, source/decision status and the chosen profile. Ensure no unauthorized source quotation, making instruction, optional-feature residue, history payload or private path survived.

Inspect desktop and mobile, overflow, typography, keyboard/links, script-disabled reading, native open/closed details and complete print output. Verify each visual node and its depth have the same step names/order/content. Test copy/AI-context equality only when that feature is selected. Test the actual recipient viewer when access exists; otherwise report that limit precisely.

Return the usable artifact and necessary source/alternate files, with the real checked scope and any material remaining limitation. Storage, a beautiful preview and publication are distinct results.
