# Draft messages

Use for recognizable messages and rewriting, including typo variants. A code/document editing request does not become a message task merely because it says “rewrite”. Specialist branches such as discovery questionnaires, collaborative article work or explicit translation should be loaded only when relevant and actually available.

Resolve the target owner/context from the request; the skill installation is not a data source selector. Read the selected root's `AGENTS.md` and optional `.agents/skill-adapters/draft.md`. The adapter owns actual sender/channel identity, personal aliases, brand/signature, writing-style sources and storage/delivery policy. Missing necessary identity/provider blocks that dependent effect, not an otherwise clear rewrite using supplied facts. Do not fall back from Work to private people/context.

## One communicative goal

Identify what this recipient should understand, choose or do. Keep only questions, requests, details and disclosure needed for that goal or explicitly requested by the user. Available background information is not an instruction to add administrative, legal or financial requests.

For rewriting, stay close to supplied wording and tone while fixing language, clarity and order. A tactical check does not authorize replacing the entire voice. Do not reintroduce a question the user removed unless they ask or a concrete necessary dependency arises; explain that dependency rather than silently adding it.

A message should be human and relevant, not a report of the assistant's internal analysis. Select detail by current significance, certainty and time horizon. Keep uncertain assumptions uncertain; a global proposal need not become a precise calendar/calculation. Exact actionable dates, amounts and references matter when they are actually needed. Resolve irrelevant arithmetic and edge cases internally instead of opening a new discussion for the recipient.

## Source and recipient sensitivity

Before each shown version, compare the source audience and intended recipient: may this content and its provenance be shared? Check identities, confidential context and unintended consequences for people mentioned. Keep routine review invisible; clarify only a material missing share/identity decision. Do not infer shareability from possession of a source or from the user's request to summarize it.

Do not invent personal facts, recipient names/addresses, relationships, commitments, motives or sources. A drafted statement is a proposal until the user accepts it; storage and a later question are not approval or sending evidence.

## Channel, sender and identity

Use explicit channel instructions first, then the actual thread/context. A reply preserves the existing subject and thread when known. Ask only when channel or identity ambiguity materially changes the result. Do not force contact enrichment for an ordinary rewrite with enough supplied facts.

Use confirmed identities from the applicable adapter: whether the user writes personally, another named sender is requested or an assistant identity is required. Never assume a generic skill may impersonate a different person or use a business account for a personal message. Resolve delivery details through the real selected channel route only when a draft setup/send effect needs them.

Email, direct chat, team chat and self-messages have different needs. A short conversation reply need not have document headings or a signature; a complete mail should have an appropriate greeting and one closing/signature. Apply actual local preferences rather than a universal name, family alias, account, language or sign-off.

## Facts and findable references

Use available relevant customer, contract, invoice, dossier or order references when they help the recipient identify the matter. Verify type, value and owner in the real source; do not call a customer ID a contract ID. In a new message the subject may carry the reference; in a reply preserve the thread subject and add it in the body when useful.

Choose necessary data first and then write known actionable values explicitly. Do not use vague “the account/reference/amount” where the recipient needs the actual value. Do not include unrelated identifiers merely because they are available, and never fabricate a reference to make the mail appear complete.

Check that the chosen reference really appears in the saved subject/body. Internal source corrections and search details stay outside the recipient's text unless needed for their action.

## Compose

1. Read the relevant style guide and examples from the selected adapter, if one exists. Existing confirmed feedback outranks a generic preference.
2. Establish sender, recipient and channel with the necessary known facts. Keep ambiguous fields visibly unresolved outside a ready-to-send block.
3. Write the central message in the right scale and voice. Preserve useful technical terms; language dictionaries are contextual advice, not blind replacements.
4. Use a suitable format from output when a formatted artifact/mail is selected. A short rewrite or chat stays directly usable text. Explicit text-only controls the delivery.
5. Use a meaningful visual when that makes reading faster. One coherent visual can carry time, steps, comparisons or actions with explanation placed at its actual part. Remove an accompanying list that says the same thing. A requested standalone block must make sense without the surrounding mail.
6. Keep the original attachments for a forward when provided and relevant; distinguish mentioning an attachment from actually attaching it.

For a first service-provider enquiry, a natural order is discovery/context when known, whether they can help in the relevant region, the actual situation, then relevant price/availability questions. Do not make every follow-up mail follow that first-contact format or disclose a full address when region is enough.

## Tactical and final review

Apply the available [master-tactician](../master-tactician/PROCEDURE.md) review proportionally: brief for routine text, deeper for negotiation, employer/client, permission, confidential or consequential matters. It is a review of honest communication, not permission to conceal a material truth or add new requests.

Check whether the draft creates unintended conditions, unnecessary disclosure, hard future promises or a misleading interpretation. Preserve the user's actual question and recipients. After substantive feedback recheck the concrete final text rather than relying on an earlier verdict.

Before every preview verify:

- one goal and only relevant exact data;
- correct identity, recipient, sender, source audience and shareability;
- preserved facts, uncertainty and commitments;
- natural language and user voice;
- no needless duplicate explanation, heading or ending;
- actual selected format and attachment state.

Fix what fails. Do not append a generic CHECK/status line to the recipient's text. Use a brief outside-the-draft note only for a meaningful content choice or missing fact.

## Present, retain and follow up

Return one usable version. For a full formatted email, follow the selected output-mail route: actual body, separate recipient/subject fields, text fallback and images where used. Unknown addresses are not invented and an existing reply subject is not replaced for convenience. Do not automatically ask about sending, clipboard or opening an app after a rewrite.

Use the selected local storage contract to retain original input, the exact shown version, feedback and later corrections, without overwriting earlier versions. State a missing required persistence capability honestly; the source of storage authority is the actual request/standing contract, not this generic skill. Never claim that a silently improved saved version was what the user saw.

Drafting, setting up a provider draft, sending, publication and confirmed receipt remain distinct effects. Work/private channel restrictions stay with their real adapter; a local human-only-email policy must be obeyed even if a send tool exists. Only an explicit authorized channel action proceeds to the available delivery skill. Preserve actual readback and do not infer sending from silence, storage or preview.
