# Vacancy quality

Evaluate open status, personal suitability and negotiable terms separately.

## Open status

Read the concrete role on the employer's official list or authorized applicant-tracking system. Verify an active application path for that same role: an actual current form, or an explicit invitation to apply by a published address. A generic contact button, open application, talent pool or a form for a different role does not suffice. Closed, filled or expired jobs stay excluded even when their page returns HTTP 200. Inaccessibility remains unverified.

Keep the actual role URL, application URL or stated address, meaningful source passages, verification date/time zone and an inspectable source copy where preservation is authorized. Recheck before applying. The target adapter may define an evidence freshness interval; without one, do not invent a universal service guarantee.

## Fit and feasibility

Use the current person's authorized criteria and evidence, not defaults from an earlier candidate. Check employer, actual duties, hard entry requirements, hours, salary period/hour basis, role location, mandatory travel and the real application route. A salary range overlapping the desired amount is not an offer at that amount.

For travel limits, verify the actual role address and relevant working times with an appropriate route source. A station-to-station journey, unrelated headquarters or the word hybrid is not a commute result. For remote roles, verify that this exact role may be performed from the relevant country and identify any office visits, customer travel or schedule constraints. Do not invent travel minutes or publish a private home address in a route link.

Unproven hard requirements remain a question to resolve, not a recommendation already released. Contract duration, pension or negotiable details can remain explicit discussion items unless the person made them a hard requirement.

## Gaps and release

A missing advertised closing time is a dated finding, not permission to invent a deadline or assume permanent openness. Preserve inclusive/exclusive wording and the source time zone. Technical metadata is not automatically the employer's confirmed closing date.

Investigate feasible roles before chasing a numeric target. For a material gap record which sources were actually read, what remains unknown and a concrete next check. One failed fetch or a full list of leads is not a completed search. Separate usable, needs-discussion, archived, outside-criteria and closed results. If a numeric requested selection cannot be met, report the actual usable count and the precise remaining gap; rejected leads do not satisfy the target.

A validator may check consistency and source-copy integrity; it does not establish source truth or guarantee that a job remains unfilled. Contacting employers, applying and publishing require their own authorization.
