# Financial reporting

Select the requested period, scope, currency, reporting basis and actual content owner. Read its current model and existing report before calculating or changing anything. Separate actual observed flows, a planned budget and conditional forecasts; a bank cash-flow extract is not a complete household balance sheet.

Read `.agents/skill-adapters/research.md` from the explicitly selected target root when present. It owns real accounts, category definitions, routing choices, source-store access, helper commands, permissions, revision conventions and storage destinations. Do not infer those from this skill's installation or a historical sample. Missing account coverage, model contracts or source/writer authority blocks the dependent result or write. Do not create a competing ledger, bypass an exclusive writer, access another vault as fallback, or edit canonical records directly.

## Establish evidence

For each account and period, choose exactly one dated export set and preserve its source identity, hash, observation date, period and balance coverage. Inspect a new bank format before interpreting columns or sign direction. Internal transfers must not be counted as new external income or spending. Without opening balances, do not claim a derived closing balance. Store only necessary financial detail in the authorized private target.

For a budget, retain the existing item definitions and dated inputs. A prior figure can remain a clearly labeled provisional planning basis unless contradicted; absence of a recent debit does not make an annual expense unknown. Recent instalments are not automatically a new annual monthly average. Ordinary purchases are not recurring subscriptions without evidence. Do not count a salary component twice merely because a payslip itemises it.

Unknown values are null, never zero. Keep assumptions, source facts and user decisions distinguishable. Ask only for missing balances, accounts or positions that existing chosen sources cannot answer. Do not manufacture financial rights, tax relief or eligibility from an old analysis. For new externally governed tax, allowance or legal-financial calculations, check current primary sources and record the relevant date/version.

## Calculate and compare

Use the selected model's formulas and stable item IDs. Keep matching identity separate from an amount becoming newly known. Compare only compatible scope, basis, currency, period and formula/category versions. An unsupported crosswalk is a limitation, not permission to compare incompatible totals silently.

Forecast from an explicit baseline and independently dated events. Income changes, leave, relocation, bridge financing, sale, family changes and childcare may have different dates and uncertainties; do not collapse them into a single assumed event. A sensitivity scenario may exclude an unknown cost only when that exclusion is prominently labeled. Report known subtotals and missing coverage instead of a definitive disposable balance from an incomplete budget. Do not label unallocated amounts as free cash without the owner's routing policy.

For assets, debts and liquidity, count each account once and identify cash basis, valuation date and debt sign conventions. Keep source dates visible: an old statement or indicative offer is historical evidence, not a fresh balance.

## Report and preserve

Lead with the result and its coverage boundary, followed by key figures and a meaningful comparison. Keep necessary source/model evidence close enough to inspect. Distinguish new items from changed numeric values and retain per-field source, reason, date and certainty. A revision changes figures; a presentation version changes form. Follow the adapter's actual naming and writer contract rather than imposing a new numbering scheme.

Retain earlier frozen inputs, reports and user comments. Create a new revision when correcting a frozen output; never overwrite a financial source merely to repair navigation. Use supported deterministic calculation and comparison tools where available. A passing schema check does not establish that an export is complete or the conclusions are financially sound.

A requested standalone report must retain enough input, formulas and source evidence for its intended recipient to inspect without relying on private file paths or unavailable networks. Full financial sources remain confidential: embed or share them only within the explicitly authorized audience and storage policy. Make no report public as an export side effect.

Before completion, reconcile relevant totals and changes to their inputs, inspect the rendered artifact when one is requested, verify the selected writer's readback, and state material missing coverage. This procedure authorizes no banking transaction, transfer, account change, new scheduler or external delivery.
