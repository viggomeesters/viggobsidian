# Source-backed research

Resolve the question, intended decision, meaningful criteria and requested output from the user's instructions and supplied sources. Ask only for information that can materially change scope or conclusion. A simple factual question needs a direct answer, not a full report; a consequential decision needs suitable breadth and counterevidence.

## Sources and prior context

Read supplied documents and relevant existing research before starting over. Check the age, assumptions and changed purpose of previous findings. Search only relevant owners rather than treating an entire vault as a default corpus.

If a task explicitly selects a target vault, read its authority and reader contracts. Check that target's `.agents/skill-adapters/research.md` when present for local criteria and verified source providers. Never infer the target from this skill's installation directory. A missing adapter blocks an owner-specific lookup or write that needs it, not independent public research. Storage is a separate effect unless the user's governing instructions already authorize it.

Use the canonical buying or travel specialization when that is the actual task. A missing specialization does not justify inventing its methods, commands or unavailable account access; explain the precise dependent gap and continue independent research where useful.

## Investigate and challenge

1. Search from the decisive questions and plausible alternatives. Search results are discovery aids; open the original source for claims used in the conclusion.
2. Prefer primary sources, official technical documentation and original publications for technical facts. Independent expertise and firsthand experience can answer different questions; identify their role and possible interests. Official marketing is not independent proof of quality.
3. Check publication/change date, jurisdiction, version, method, scope and applicable conditions. Compare conflicting sources and state what remains undecidable.
4. Distinguish verified fact, commercial claim, interpretation, estimate and advice. Calculations retain units, assumptions, inputs and an independent check when material.
5. Verify changeable information currently: prices, availability, specifications, opening status and terms. Do not describe an unreachable page or a snippet as a live verification. Use relevant periods in searches rather than a permanently hardcoded year.
6. Use counterevidence to test the recommendation. Report the consequence of missing evidence instead of filling gaps with confident prose or arbitrary scores.

## Prices and exact variants

Verify the actual product/offer page, variant, quantity, currency and verification date. Include material delivery charges, taxes and other total-price components. Unknown costs remain unknown; when price verification failed, say so. A category page, marketplace title or availability badge for another variant is insufficient.

## Vacancies and other time-sensitive selections

For job selection use [vacancy quality](references/vacancy-quality.md). Actual open status, fit against the current person's verified criteria, and negotiable employment terms are separate questions. Subject-specific salary, travel, role or count requirements belong to the selected private/work adapter or owner, not to this general procedure. A completed evidence checklist or HTTP success does not prove a usable recommendation.

## Requested research frameworks

If the user names a particular framework, read its actual reference before claiming to apply it. If unavailable, identify that limitation and offer the source-backed method here. When that exact framework is a hard requirement, ask for its missing reference rather than inventing it.

## Deliver

Lead with the answer/recommendation and the decisive reason. Support material claims with direct links near the claim. Give relevant tradeoffs, uncertainty and a concrete decision rule. Use a comparison table only where criteria and differences benefit from one. A long report or durable artifact is appropriate when requested, not a mandatory byproduct of research.

Mention owner context only where it materially affects the answer. Keep confidential context out of a shareable result unless explicitly intended for its audience. A source count or long bibliography does not prove completeness.

For authorized preservation, use the selected owner's writer, keep original sources/date/assumptions/uncertainty and update the existing dossier where appropriate. No second note store, automatic daily write, purchase, application, outbound contact or publication follows from a research request alone.
