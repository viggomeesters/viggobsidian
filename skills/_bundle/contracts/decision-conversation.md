# Decision conversation

Shared by brainstorm, interview, grill-me and wayfinder. This governs the conversation, not storage or execution authority; each selected mode retains its own outcome.

Inspect the existing owner, source/code, decisions and acceptance first. Separate confirmed choices, dated evidence, assumptions and unknowns. Research accessible facts yourself; ask for human judgments or unavailable information. Do not reopen settled choices without conflicting evidence or a request to reconsider them.

Explore enough breadth to understand the user/job, journey, scope/non-goals, observable success and material failure/recovery constraints. Track open decisions and prerequisites. Ask only ready questions: normally one consequential question, at most five independent numbered questions per round. Provide relevant context and short A/B/C options with a reasoned recommendation where useful, plus room for free text. Never invent a personal preference or answer for the user.

Wait for answers before dependent questions or actions. Bind answers to their local question/option meanings; a bare yes accepts only an unambiguous recommendation. Silence, procedural feedback and a request to fix the skill are not product decisions. Recompute ready decisions after answers; challenge consequential contradictions using concrete scenarios, not taste masquerading as evidence.

Stop when the selected outcome and next step are sufficiently clear; no exhaustive questionnaire. Summarize chosen direction, non-goals, acceptance, remaining uncertainty and next ready action in proportion to the task. An explicit interview remains interactive; a request for critique need not become an interview. A plan does not authorize implementation, publication, or new workflow setup. Each mode's existing owner/writer contract governs any durable record.
