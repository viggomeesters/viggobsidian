# Buying comparison

This is the buying branch of [research](../research/PROCEDURE.md), not a second research method or renderer. Use the full current question, supplied constraints and exact intended use. Reuse confirmed details; ask only for missing information that materially changes the choice. Advice does not authorize checkout, contact, payment, publication or creating a purchase record.

When a target is explicitly selected, read its `.agents/skill-adapters/shop.md` if present for applicable personal/category preferences and verified compatibility context. Do not load unrelated product dossiers or infer preferences from another person. Never choose a target vault from the installed skill's location. A missing required compatibility source limits the claim; it does not justify guessing a specification.

## Compare actual options

Use research's source and currentness checks. Verify the exact purchasable variant, material dimensions/interfaces/platform support, quantity and condition. Manufacturer marketing, snippets, category pages and copied marketplace titles are not independent evidence of actual compatibility or stock.

Compare the criteria that drive the actual decision: adequate capability, compatibility, reliable quality/service, expected lifetime/repairability where relevant, and total delivered cost. Show delivery charges, taxes and other mandatory cost components or mark them unknown. State date/variant/source for changeable price or availability. Keep fact, commercial claim, interpretation and recommendation distinct.

Explain the primary choice and the decision rule that would change it. Present meaningful quality, value or cheapest-adequate alternatives only where those are real distinct choices; do not manufacture a fixed number of recommendations or use arbitrary star scores. Call out avoidable mismatches and material unknowns. A user may prefer the cheapest sufficient option over a premium one; their criteria govern.

## Deliver and preserve

A concise chat answer is usually sufficient. For a requested durable guide, use the current output/deliverable owner and explicit chosen format. The guide should retain the original question, core recommendation, decision criteria, variant/source binding, exact shop links and actual total prices. Do not impose a historical multi-screen layout, runtime, mandatory archive ZIP or visual rendering on a chat-only recommendation.

If HTML is requested, its own maker checks desktop/mobile, actual loaded assets and readable price information. Photos must match the real variant and permitted source. A local guide is not a public page, successful purchase or actual stock reservation.

Preserve sources only under the selected owner's actual storage authorization and supported writer. No automatic daily entry, parallel dossier or publication follows from a comparison. Legacy exports may remain readable without dictating the format of new work.
