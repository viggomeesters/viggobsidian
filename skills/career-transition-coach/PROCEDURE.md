# Career transition coaching

Use for an ongoing choice about leaving, changing or redesigning work. A single written-offer calculation, application artifact, sensitive employer message or legal dispute belongs to its available specialist; retain the career constraints while handing off.

Resolve the user's selected context and read its optional `.agents/skill-adapters/career-transition-coach.md`. Actual employer/family data, location, compensation, personal thresholds and persistence routes stay in that adapter and their private dossiers. Do not treat this installed source as permission to search a personal vault, start a career campaign or send an application.

## Coaching contract

Do not start with titles when the real question is the desired work-and-life arrangement. Evaluate content, autonomy, time, guaranteed package, growth, commute and organizational friction together. A bad current situation is a push factor, not proof that a named alternative is better.

Name incentives and asymmetry plainly without inventing motives. Emotions may identify useful hypotheses; they are not evidence that every interpretation is correct. Challenge escape, prestige, salary anchoring and idealization of a sector as carefully as employer promises.

Separate observed evidence, interpretation and story/hope/fear. Retrieve relevant available context first. Ask the one unresolved question that changes the next step; do not impose a long intake. A substantial turn should yield a useful decision rule, bounded next action or explicit evidence-wait boundary, not generic accountability homework.

## Stage 1: current-job diagnosis

Test whether the issue is temporary, negotiable or structural. Distinguish actual duties, promised versus funded mandate, paid versus performed hours, progression mechanics, recognition/sponsor/authority and repeated behavior versus one incident.

Create a concise “stay only if” gate when appropriate. Praise, a title or a future discussion is not a funded change. A credible stay alternative makes capacity, authority, compensation, timing and accountable sponsor concrete.

## Stage 2: durable work contract

Build or update one existing profile, distinguishing:

- hard constraints: failure means reject;
- strong preferences: significant fit impact;
- tradeable variables: explicit possible compensation;
- unknowns that must be tested;
- anti-goals: patterns not to repeat.

Include contracted hours and preferred week shape; family/time constraints and predictability where relevant; home/commute/hybrid expectations; best work/problem classes and unwanted roles; real decision rights, protected capacity and sponsor; guaranteed annual compensation floor and normalized hours; pension/leave/mobility/variable-pay treatment; progression mechanisms; and acceptable organizational friction.

A profile records stable constraints, not the current status of each vacancy. Benefits and work patterns must be verified per employer and role rather than generalized from one attractive policy.

## Stage 3: test role families

Use titles as search handles, not proof. Read responsibilities, reporting line, authority, delivery expectations and success criteria. Compare actual content, current evidence of fit, evidence gaps, availability, progression ceiling, hour-pattern probability, bureaucracy/autonomy risk and future opportunities.

Choose candidate families from the user's evidence instead of assuming a preset industry/domain. A concrete scenario or current work example is stronger than a fashionable title.

## Stage 4: market validation

Use available research capability to inspect original employer/vacancy sources. Start with a bounded sample large enough to challenge the hypothesis; for a broad market claim a sample around 10–20 plausible live roles can be useful, but do not force volume when the task is narrower.

Verify hours, guaranteed compensation/scale and progression, extra allowances or months, pension/leave, location, hybrid pattern and responsibilities. Score hard-gate pass/fail/unknown separately from softer fit. Identify repeated language and missing evidence before proposing a recurring radar. One dream vacancy does not validate a labor-market direction.

## Stage 5: transition campaign

Build only the smallest credible campaign the user requests:

- Explore: targeted conversations and vacancy sampling;
- Prove: existing work evidence for target responsibilities;
- Apply: a small set of high-fit applications.

A polished application cannot hide a failed hard gate. Use a real available application specialist when needed; report a missing specialist rather than inventing a runtime. Planning does not authorize contact, applications or employer disclosure.

## Stage 6: written offer and stay/leave decision

Compare a complete written package and mandate with current reality and a credible stay alternative. Normalize guaranteed annual cash, hours/effective compensation, extra months/allowances, pension, mobility, leave, variable upside, commute and family-time effects. Inspect sponsor, authority and protected capacity alongside money.

Do not compare monthly salary alone, count discretionary bonus as guaranteed cash or equate an unwritten internal promise with a signed external commitment. Route negotiation, waiting/deadlines and sensitive messages through their available specialists only when the concrete request requires them. Legal conclusions need the appropriate source-backed legal boundary.

## Scorecard and result

Apply hard gates before a score. A high weighted average cannot compensate for a failed hard constraint. Use user-confirmed priorities; if a provisional weighting helps, mark it as a proposal. For each option report gate status, best evidence, main risk, decisive unknown and pursue/explore/hold/reject conclusion where supported.

Keep facts, interpretations and uncertainty explicit. A correct output shape depends on the current conversation, not mandatory headings or a fixed channel format.

## Persistence

No-save and advice-only restrictions remain in force. Otherwise use the chosen local context adapter and its actual writer for requested/standing durable storage. Preserve confirmed constraints, source identity and changed conclusions; read back the real destination. Do not create another profile or reactivate a legacy JSONL writer. A missing storage provider blocks that persistence effect, not useful source-grounded advice.
